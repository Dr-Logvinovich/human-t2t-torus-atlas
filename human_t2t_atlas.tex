\begin{abstract}
We present a deterministic, zero-random-number-generator (Zero-RNG) whole-chromosome structural audit of the complete gapless human assembly T2T-CHM13v2.0 (\texttt{GCF\_009914755.1}; 24 chromosomes, $3.117\text{ Gb}$, $3{,}117{,}275{,}381$ adjacent triplet pairs at lag $d=3\text{ bp}$). Representing consecutive nucleotide triplets on the discrete torus $\Ttorus = (\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z})^3$, we apply the exact Klein four-group $V_4 = \{I, T, R, S\}$ orthogonal decomposition separating Chargaff PR2 symmetry from directional $5'\!\to 3'$ irreversibility, together with a $V_4$-closed $496$-dimensional dinucleotide screen envelope $\Pienv$ and its $3600$-dimensional complement. Using a data-driven heteroscedastic Kittler--Illingworth BIC partition on $\log_{10}(\Inbraw)$ constrained by entropy inversion ($0.0\%$ clipping across all 24 chromosomes; $\dBIC \in [294.5, 2527.0]$), the genome cleanly bifurcates into euchromatin ($28{,}130$ 100-kb sectors, $90.27\%$) and satellite/tandem firmware ($3{,}031$ sectors, $9.73\%$). Across the pooled $2.813\text{ Gb}$ euchromatin compartment at $d=3\text{ bp}$, directional flux is $99.9995\%$ PR2-compliant ($\|\Wmp\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}} = 1.22\times 10^{-5}$ vs.\ $\|\Wmm\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}} = 5.86\times 10^{-11}$), $86.4\%$ CpG-free (with all top-15 canonical directed 6-mer orbits strictly CpG-free, led by \texttt{GGG-AGG/CCT-CCC} at $2.12\%$), and $37.0\%$ orthogonal to the $V_4$-closed screen envelope. Every chromosome passes leave-one-block-out 1-Mb Newey--West HAC cluster inference ($t_{\mathrm{HAC}} \in [19.56, 112.73]$) and retains $100.00\%$ residual energy against an exact $M=1000\text{ bp}$ compositional-current null. Across lags $d \in \{3,\dots,171\}\text{ bp}$, we prove exact $\bmod\,p$ tandem-repeat Klein aliasing explaining the apparent whole-genome rebound at $d=147$ and $171\text{ bp}$ via HSat2/3 $(\texttt{GGAAT})_n$ ($p=5$), while satellite-stripped euchromatin eliminates the $d=147/171\text{ bp}$ rebound completely, exhibiting a helical-turn shoulder at $d=9\text{--}10\text{ bp}$ ($2.97\times 10^{-6}$) followed by smooth monotonic decay across $d=10\text{--}171\text{ bp}$ down to $1.43\times 10^{-7}$ (with strictly monotonic $\Inb$ decay across all 10 lags, power-law scaling $\|\Wmp\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}}(d)\propto d^{-0.998}$ [$R^2=0.940$], and $\ge 99.99\%$ PR2 compliance throughout). Cross-chromosomally, euchromatic arrows align at $95.82\%$ coherence along an effectively 1D autosomal GC/Alu manifold (PC1 $=87.97\%$, $\mathrm{PR}=1.29$) while chiral PR2 defects cancel $94.4\times$; spaced palindromes $(u,\rho(u))$ are proved algebraically PR2-locked with $\ge 56.25\%$ universal non-envelope escape ($87.89\%$ per mode), and at $d=171\text{ bp}$ the residual euchromatic arrow condenses onto the canonical Alu \texttt{CTCTACT}$\to$\texttt{AAA} dipole ($25.36\%$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematical Framework (Theorems D1--D19)}
\label{sec:framework}
% =====================================================================

Let $s = (s_0,\dots,s_{L-1}) \in \{0,1,2,3\}^L$ encode \texttt{A,C,G,T}. Each contiguous 3-mer maps to a vertex $u \in \Ttorus = (\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z})^3$ ($|\Ttorus|=64$), with Watson--Crick reverse-complement involution $\rho(u) = \overline{u}^{\mathrm{rev}}$. For lag $d \ge 1$, let $B^{(d)}_{u,v}$ count valid triplet pairs $(c_i, c_{i+d}) = (u,v)$ summed across all three reading phases without crossing gaps, and set $M^{(d)} = B^{(d)} / N$ with $N = \sum_{u,v} B^{(d)}_{u,v}$.

\subsection{Klein $V_4$ Decomposition and Unbiased Energies (D1, D2, D9)}
Two commuting isometric involutions act on $\mathbb{R}^{64\times 64}$: transposition $T(M) = M^\top$ (reversing $5'\!\to 3'$ order) and reverse-complement conjugation $R(M)_{u,v} = M_{\rho(v),\rho(u)}$ (swapping strands). Together with $S = TR$, they generate the Klein four-group $V_4 = \{I, T, R, S\}$, inducing the exact Frobenius-orthogonal splitting
\begin{equation}
  M = \Wpp + \Wpm + \Wmp + \Wmm, \qquad
  \|M\|_F^2 = \|\Wpp\|_F^2 + \|\Wpm\|_F^2 + \|\Wmp\|_F^2 + \|\Wmm\|_F^2,
  \label{eq:klein}
\end{equation}
where the subscripts denote $(T,R)$ eigenvalues $\in \{+1,-1\}^2$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\Wpp$ ($T=+1, R=+1$, dim $1056$): static, PR2-symmetric background;
  \item $\Wpm$ ($T=+1, R=-1$, dim $1024$): static PR2 defect (strand-composition skew);
  \item $\Wmp$ ($T=-1, R=+1$, dim $1024$): \textbf{PR2-compliant directional arrow} ($5'\!\to 3'$ irreversibility invariant under reverse complementation);
  \item $\Wmm$ ($T=-1, R=-1$, dim $992$): chiral PR2-violating directional flow.
\end{itemize}
\begin{theorem}[Klein $V_4$ Splitting and Heterogeneity Annihilation (D1--D2)]
\label{thm:klein_het}
For any sequence $s$ and lag $d\ge 1$, the reverse-complement sequence $\mathrm{rc}(s)$ satisfies $B^{(d)}(\mathrm{rc}(s)) = R(B^{(d)}(s))$. Consequently, any within-sector independent-marginal null $E_{\mathrm{het}}^{(d)} = \sum_w \alpha_w (c_w c_w^\top - \mathrm{diag}(c_w))$ is strictly $T$-symmetric, so $\Wmp(E_{\mathrm{het}}^{(d)}) = \Wmm(E_{\mathrm{het}}^{(d)}) = 0$ identically.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Proof.} Each rank-one summand $c_w c_w^\top$ and diagonal matrix $\mathrm{diag}(c_w)$ satisfies $T(A)=A^\top=A$; since $\Wmp$ and $\Wmm$ lie in the $(-1)$-eigenspace of $T$, their projections onto $E_{\mathrm{het}}^{(d)}$ vanish identically. $\square$

\begin{corollary}[Compositional vs.\ Chiral PR2 Splitting]
\label{cor:pr2_dyn}
Any pure strand-composition skew $\delta p_u = -\delta p_{\rho(u)}$ perturbs the independent-pair tensor $p\otimes p$ by $\delta p\otimes p + p\otimes \delta p$, which is strictly $T$-symmetric and $R$-antisymmetric; hence compositional PR2 defects inject $100\%$ into static $\Wpm$ and $0\%$ into chiral $\Wmm$. Conversely, an off-diagonal unidirectional tandem transition $E_{u,v}$ ($u\neq v, v\neq\rho(u)$) projects with equal Frobenius weight ($1/4$) onto all four Klein quadrants in Eq.~\eqref{eq:klein}, driving the dynamic PR2 share $\mathrm{PR2}_{\mathrm{dyn}} := \|\Wmm\|^2 / (\|\Wpm\|^2 + \|\Wmm\|^2)$ toward $50\%$.
\end{corollary}

\noindent To remove $O(1/N)$ multinomial sampling bias on finite sectors and pooled strata, \textbf{Theorem D9} (Theorem~\ref{thm:orbit_debias}) computes exact $U$-statistic unbiased squared norms $\|\Wmp\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}}$ and $\|\Wmm\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}}$ from the four $V_4$ inner products corrected by the homodimer diagonal $p_{\mathrm{diag}}$ and 6-bp palindrome trace $p_{\mathrm{pal}}$.

\subsection{Two-Sided Screen and $V_4$-Closed Envelope (D3, D11, D13)}
Denoting the suffix dinucleotide of $u$ by $\mathrm{suf}(u) \in \{0,\dots,15\}$ and the prefix dinucleotide of $v$ by $\mathrm{pre}(v) \in \{0,\dots,15\}$, \textbf{Theorem D3} splits total triplet mutual information exactly into four nonnegative terms ($\mathrm{df}=225+720+720+2304=3969$):
\begin{equation}
  I(X;Y) = I_{\mathrm{bridge}} + \underbrace{D_{5'\text{-lead}} + D_{3'\text{-wobble}} + D_{1,3}}_{\Inbraw}, \qquad
  \Inb = \Inbraw - \frac{\mathrm{df}_{\mathrm{nonbr}}}{2N}.
\end{equation}
Analogously, comparing within-sector pair counts $B_{\mathrm{within}}^{(d)}$ against the heterogeneity null $E_{\mathrm{het}}^{(d)}$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:klein_het}) via the exact screen-stratified KL chain rule yields the Miller--Madow-debiased non-bridge divergence $K_{\mathrm{nonbr}}^{\mathrm{MM}} = D_{\mathrm{KL}}(B_{\mathrm{within}}\|E_{\mathrm{het}}) - K_{\mathrm{bridge}} - \mathrm{df}_{\mathrm{nonbr}}/(2N)$.
Let $D_S$ and $D_O$ be the orthogonal averaging projectors onto $\mathrm{suf}$ and $\mathrm{pre}$ fibers, and $D_{C2} = D_S D_O$. Because the naive one-sided bridge projector $M \mapsto D_S M D_O$ does not commute with $T$, \textbf{Theorem D13} constructs the minimal $V_4$-closed screen envelope projector
\begin{equation}
  \Pienv(M) = D_S M D_O + D_O M D_S - D_{C2} M D_{C2},
  \label{eq:pienv}
\end{equation}
which yields the following exact $V_4$-equivariant decomposition.

\begin{theorem}[Minimal $V_4$-Closed Dinucleotide Screen Envelope (D13)]
\label{thm:pienv}
The operator $\Pienv$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:pienv} is an orthogonal projector ($\Pienv^2 = \Pienv = \Pienv^\dagger$) commuting with every element of $V_4 = \{I,T,R,S\}$. Its image has exact dimension $496$ splitting across $(\Wpp,\Wpm,\Wmp,\Wmm)$ as $132 \oplus 118 \oplus 130 \oplus 116$, and its orthogonal complement $(I-\Pienv)$ has dimension $3600$ ($924 \oplus 906 \oplus 894 \oplus 876$) with zero cross-quadrant leakage.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Proof.} Since $D_S$ and $D_O$ are symmetric commuting block-averaging projectors ($D_S D_O = D_O D_S = D_{C2}$) exchanged by transposition ($T(D_S M D_O) = D_O M^\top D_S$) and preserved under reverse complementation ($R(D_S M D_O) = D_S R(M) D_O$), both $D_S(\cdot)D_O + D_O(\cdot)D_S$ and $D_{C2}(\cdot)D_{C2}$ commute with $T$ and $R$, hence with all of $V_4$. Direct character traces give the stated quadrant ranks (verified to $<5\times 10^{-16}$). $\square$

\subsection{Data-Driven Satellite Partition, Persistence, and Aliasing (D4--D8, D14--D19)}
\begin{theorem}[Exact $\bmod\,p$ Tandem-Repeat Klein Aliasing (D19)]
\label{thm:aliasing}
Let $s$ be an ideal periodic tandem repeat of minimal period $p\ge 1$ ($s_{i+p}=s_i$). Whenever $d \equiv 0 \pmod p$, every triplet pair satisfies $c_{i+d}=c_i$, so $B^{(d)}$ is diagonal and $\Wmp(B^{(d)}) = \Wmm(B^{(d)}) = 0$. Conversely, at any lag $d \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$ with non-palindromic phase shift, $\Wmp(B^{(d)}) \neq 0$ re-emerges at full tandem amplitude.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Proof.} Diagonal matrices are fixed by $T$, annihilating $\Wmp \oplus \Wmm$; for $d \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$, $B^{(d)}$ is supported on the directed shift $c_i \mapsto c_{i+d}$ whose transpose corresponds to lag $-d \not\equiv d \pmod p$ whenever $2d \not\equiv 0 \pmod p$. $\square$

\begin{theorem}[Palindromic Anti-Diagonal Chirality-Free Locking and $(15/16)^2$ Screen Escape]
\label{thm:pal_lock}
Because codon length $k=3$ is odd and Watson--Crick base complementation is fixed-point-free, $\rho(u)\neq u$ for every $u\in\Ttorus$. Let $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{pal}} = \mathrm{span}\{E_{u,\rho(u)} : u\in\Ttorus\} \subset \mathbb{R}^{64\times 64}$ denote the $64$-dimensional spaced-palindrome anti-diagonal. Then:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{pal}}$ splits across $(\Wpp,\Wpm,\Wmp,\Wmm)$ as $32 \oplus 0 \oplus 32 \oplus 0$; every oriented palindrome mode $E^-_{u,\rho(u)} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(E_{u,\rho(u)} - E_{\rho(u),u})$ is algebraically locked into $\Wmp$ with identically zero PR2-violating projection ($\Wpm = \Wmm = 0$).
  \item Every basis mode $E^-_{u,\rho(u)}$ satisfies exact screen-envelope projection $\|\Pienv(E^-_{u,\rho(u)})\|_F^2 = \frac{31}{256}$ ($12.11\%$) and pure $3600$-D non-envelope escape $\|(I-\Pienv)E^-_{u,\rho(u)}\|_F^2 = \frac{225}{256} = (15/16)^2$ ($87.89\%$). 
  \item Let $Q_{\mathrm{pal}}^-$ denote orthogonal projection onto the $32$-dimensional oriented palindrome subspace $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{pal}}^- \subset \Wmp$. The compressed envelope operator $Q_{\mathrm{pal}}^-\Pienv Q_{\mathrm{pal}}^-$ has exact discrete spectrum $\{\frac{7}{16}\;(\text{mult }2),\;\frac{1}{4}\;(\text{mult }12),\;0\;(\text{mult }18)\}$. Consequently, \emph{every} nonzero palindromic arrow $X\in\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{pal}}^-$ obeys the universal lower bound $\|(I-\Pienv)X\|_F^2 \ge \frac{9}{16}\|X\|_F^2$ ($56.25\%$), and an $18$-dimensional ``dark palindromic'' kernel lies $100\%$ orthogonal to $\Pienv$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Proof.} For $u=(x,y,z)$, $\rho(u)=(\bar{z},\bar{y},\bar{x})$ satisfies $R(E_{u,\rho(u)}) = E_{\rho^2(u),\rho(u)} = E_{u,\rho(u)}$, fixing $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{pal}}$ pointwise under $R$ and annihilating $\Wpm\oplus\Wmm$, while $T$ swaps the $32$ disjoint pairs $(u,\rho(u))\leftrightarrow(\rho(u),u)$, yielding $32\oplus 32$ in $\Wpp\oplus\Wmp$. Since the middle base obeys $\bar{y}\neq y$, neither $\mathrm{suf}$, $\mathrm{pre}$, nor middle-base fibers coincide between $u$ and $\rho(u)$; all cross-terms between $E_{u,\rho(u)}$ and $E_{\rho(u),u}$ vanish under $D_S(\cdot)D_O$, $D_O(\cdot)D_S$, and $D_{C2}(\cdot)D_{C2}$. Because $\Pienv$ is an orthogonal projector, $\|\Pienv(E^-_{u,\rho(u)})\|_F^2 = \langle E_{u,\rho(u)}, \Pienv(E_{u,\rho(u)})\rangle = \frac{1}{16} + \frac{1}{16} - \frac{1}{256} = \frac{31}{256}$, leaving $1 - \frac{31}{256} = \frac{225}{256}$ in $(I-\Pienv)$. Identifying $E_{u,\rho(u)}$ with $|x,y,z\rangle\in(\mathbb{C}^4)^{\otimes 3}$ and noting that $\mathrm{suf}(u)=\mathrm{suf}(w)\Leftrightarrow\mathrm{pre}(\rho(u))=\mathrm{pre}(\rho(w))$, compression of Eq.~\eqref{eq:pienv} onto $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{pal}}$ reduces to $G = \frac{1}{4}(P_1\otimes I_2\otimes I_3 + I_1\otimes I_2\otimes P_3) - \frac{1}{16}(P_1\otimes I_2\otimes P_3)$, where $P_1,P_3$ average over outer bases $x,z$. Restricting to the $T$-antisymmetric subspace $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{pal}}^-$ (where $T$ swaps $P_1\leftrightarrow P_3$ and pairs $y\leftrightarrow\bar{y}$) gives eigenvalues $\frac{7}{16}$ (dim $2$), $\frac{1}{4}$ (dim $12$), and $0$ (dim $18$), yielding $\|Q_{\mathrm{pal}}^-\Pienv Q_{\mathrm{pal}}^-\|_{\mathrm{op}} = \frac{7}{16}$ and universal escape $\ge 1 - \frac{7}{16} = \frac{9}{16}$ (verified numerically to $<10^{-14}$). $\square$

\begin{theorem}[Exact $V_4$ Orbit Enumeration and $U$-Statistic Debiasing (D9 \& Lemma D14.2)]
\label{thm:orbit_debias}
Under the $V_4=\{I,T,R,S\}$ action on the $4096$ index pairs of $\Ttorus\times\Ttorus$, there are $32$ diagonal homodimer orbits $\{(u,u),(\rho(u),\rho(u))\}$ (size $2$, $T=+1$), $32$ palindromic anti-diagonal orbits $\{(u,\rho(u)),(\rho(u),u)\}$ (size $2$, $R=+1$), $0$ fixed points of $S$, and $992$ free generic orbits (size $4$), giving exact quadrant ranks $1056 \oplus 1024 \oplus 1024 \oplus 992 = 4096$. For $N$ multinomial draws with empirical transition matrix $M=B/N$, homodimer fraction $\hat{p}_{\mathrm{diag}}=\mathrm{tr}(M)$, and 6-bp palindrome fraction $\hat{p}_{\mathrm{pal}}=\sum_u M_{u,\rho(u)}$, the exact minimum-variance unbiased quadratic estimator for each $V_4$ quadrant $\sigma=(s_T,s_R)\in\{+1,-1\}^2$ is
\begin{equation}
  \|\widehat{W}_{\sigma}\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}}
  = \frac{N\|W_{\sigma}(M)\|_F^2 - \frac{1}{4}\bigl(1 + s_T \hat{p}_{\mathrm{diag}} + s_R \hat{p}_{\mathrm{pal}}\bigr)}{N - 1}.
  \label{eq:ustats}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Proof.} For any single draw $X_i = E_{u,v}$, $\langle X_i, T(X_i)\rangle = \mathbf{1}_{u=v}$, $\langle X_i, R(X_i)\rangle = \mathbf{1}_{v=\rho(u)}$, and $\langle X_i, S(X_i)\rangle = 0$ because $\rho(u)\neq u$. Hence $\|Q_\sigma(X_i)\|_F^2 = \frac{1}{4}(1+s_T\mathbf{1}_{u=v}+s_R\mathbf{1}_{v=\rho(u)})$. Splitting $\|Q_\sigma(M)\|_F^2 = \frac{1}{N^2}\sum_i \|Q_\sigma(X_i)\|_F^2 + \frac{1}{N^2}\sum_{i\neq j}\langle Q_\sigma(X_i), Q_\sigma(X_j)\rangle$ and taking expectations gives $\mathbb{E}[\|W_\sigma(M)\|_F^2] = \frac{N-1}{N}\|W_\sigma(P)\|_F^2 + \frac{1}{4N}(1+s_T p_{\mathrm{diag}}+s_R p_{\mathrm{pal}})$, which inverts uniquely to Eq.~\eqref{eq:ustats} (verified against engine JSON outputs to $<1.1\times 10^{-19}$). $\square$

\begin{corollary}[Finite-Sample Pooling Floor]
\label{cor:floor}
Under a null with $W_\sigma(P)=0$, the raw sampling bias floor equals $\frac{1}{4N}(1+s_T p_{\mathrm{diag}}+s_R p_{\mathrm{pal}}) = \frac{1}{4N}[1+O(10^{-2})]$; observed $\|\widehat{W}_{\sigma}\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}} \lesssim 1/(4N)$ is statistically indistinguishable from exact zero. While Eq.~\eqref{eq:ustats} removes exact $O(1/N)$ multinomial sampling bias, genomic triplet overlap and megabase autocorrelation are handled independently by Theorem~D14 (disjoint stride sweeps and 1-Mb LOO Newey--West HAC inference).
\end{corollary}

\begin{theorem}[Inter-Chromosomal Coherence Ratio and Chiral Cancellation Inequality]
\label{thm:coherence}
Let chromosomes $c=1,\dots,C$ carry positive pair-count weights $w_c>0$ ($\sum_{c=1}^C w_c = 1$) and quadrant projections $W_\sigma^{(c)}$, with grand-pooled matrix $\overline{W}_\sigma = \sum_{c=1}^C w_c W_\sigma^{(c)}$. Define the \emph{inter-chromosomal coherence ratio}
\begin{equation}
  \kappa_\sigma := \frac{\|\overline{W}_\sigma\|_F^2}{\sum_{c=1}^C w_c \|W_\sigma^{(c)}\|_F^2} \in [0, 1].
  \label{eq:coherence}
\end{equation}
Then $0 \le \kappa_\sigma \le 1$, with $\kappa_\sigma = 1$ if and only if all chromosomes share an identical tensor $W_\sigma^{(c)} \equiv \overline{W}_\sigma$, whereas $\kappa_\sigma \ll 1$ certifies destructive inter-chromosomal phase or strand-orientation cancellation.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Proof.} Direct consequence of the strict convexity of $\|\cdot\|_F^2$ via Jensen's inequality, $\|\sum_c w_c W_\sigma^{(c)}\|_F^2 = \sum_c w_c \|W_\sigma^{(c)}\|_F^2 - \sum_c w_c \|W_\sigma^{(c)} - \overline{W}_\sigma\|_F^2$. $\square$

\subsection{Theorems D12 \& D19: Multi-Lag Spectrum and Exact $\bmod\,5$ Tandem-Repeat Aliasing}
Evaluating $B^{(d)}$ across ten lags spanning the helical turn ($d=10,11\text{ bp}$), nucleosome core ($147\text{ bp}$), and $\alpha$-satellite monomer ($171\text{ bp}$) reveals a striking contrast between the unpartitioned whole genome and satellite-stripped euchromatin (Table~\ref{tab:multilag}, Figure~\ref{fig:d12}A).

On the whole genome, $\|\Wmp\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}}$ drops sharply at every lag divisible by $5$ ($d=10,15,30,90$) and rebounds strongly at $d=11$ ($6.93\times 10^{-6}$), $d=147$ ($1.59\times 10^{-6}$), and $d=171\text{ bp}$ ($2.46\times 10^{-6}$). \textbf{Theorem D19} (Theorem~\ref{thm:aliasing}) proves this is an exact arithmetic aliasing effect of the pentameric HSat2/3 repeat $(\texttt{GGAAT})_n$ ($p=5$): for any ideal period-$p$ tandem repeat, $c_{i+d}=c_i$ whenever $d \equiv 0 \pmod p$, confining $B^{(d)}$ to the $T$-symmetric diagonal ($u=v$) where antisymmetric $\Wmp=\Wmm=0$; conversely, whenever $d \not\equiv 0 \pmod 5$ ($147 \equiv 2$, $171 \equiv 1$), HSat2/3 phase shifts re-enter $\Wmp$ at full amplitude. Five phase-shifted $(\texttt{GGAAT})_n$ orbits generate $78.00\%$ of whole-genome $\|\Wmp\|^2$ at $d=147\text{ bp}$ and $82.46\%$ at $d=171\text{ bp}$.

In the complete $24$-chromosome pure-euchromatin pool ($2.813\text{ Gb}$), this $\bmod\,5$ sawtooth and the $147/171\text{ bp}$ rebound vanish completely (Table~\ref{tab:multilag}): $\|\Wmp\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}}$ drops from $1.22\times 10^{-5}$ ($d=3$) to $2.63\times 10^{-6}$ ($d=6$), shows a modest B-DNA helical-turn shoulder at $d=9\text{--}10\text{ bp}$ ($2.96\text{--}2.97\times 10^{-6}$), and then decays smoothly and monotonically to $1.43\times 10^{-7}$ at $d=171\text{ bp}$ ($>1.5\times 10^4$ above $\|\Wmm\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}} = 9.49\times 10^{-12}$, preserving $\ge 99.99\%$ PR2 compliance at every lag). Meanwhile, pure euchromatic $\Inb$ decays strictly monotonically across all ten lags ($0.02292 \to 0.00276\text{ nats}$), while the 3600-D non-envelope share climbs from $37.0\%$ ($d=3$) to $73.1\%$--$86.4\%$ for $d \ge 6\text{ bp}$. Remarkably, Theorem~\ref{thm:pal_lock} explains this abrupt spectral transition: whereas zero of the top-15 euchromatic orbits at $d=3\text{ bp}$ are palindromic, for $d \ge 6\text{ bp}$ chirality-free spaced palindromes $(u,\rho(u))$ surge to rank \#1 across $d=6$ (\texttt{CCA-TGG}, $6.47\%$ from $1/1024$ of $\Wmp$ dimensions---a $66.3\times$ dimensional concentration; top-3 palindromes \texttt{CCA-TGG}, \texttt{TTT-AAA}, \texttt{GTG-CAC} capture $8.99\%$, $30.7\times$ enrichment), $d=9$ (\texttt{CTG-CAG}, $3.15\%$, $32.3\times$), $d=11$ (\texttt{CCC-GGG}, $1.78\%$), and $d=15\text{ bp}$ (\texttt{CAG-CTG}, $1.95\%$, displaying an exact phase-dependent orientation reversal relative to \texttt{CTG-CAG} at $d=9$ and $30\text{ bp}$). Because every palindromic arrow mode carries $(15/16)^2 = 87.89\%$ single-mode escape and $\ge 56.25\%$ universal escape (Theorem~\ref{thm:pal_lock}), their emergence algebraically drives the jump in pure 3600-D non-envelope syntax at $d \ge 6\text{ bp}$. (Note two exact accounting conventions at $d\ge 6\text{ bp}$: orbits are ranked by single-entry flux amplitude $|(\Wmp)_{u,v}|$, under which size-2 palindromes lead $d=6,9,11,15\text{ bp}$, whereas by total orbit energy $m_{\mathrm{orb}}w^2$, size-4 orbits \texttt{CTC-CTG} [$4.75\%$], \texttt{GGT-CCT} [$3.19\%$], and \texttt{TCC-ATT} [$3.13\%$] exceed the size-2 palindromes at $d=9,11,15\text{ bp}$ while \texttt{CCA-TGG} [$6.47\%$] leads under both criteria at $d=6\text{ bp}$; and the CpG-free filter conservatively excludes concatenated junction \texttt{C|G} pairs even across $d-3\text{ bp}$ spacers, making the $86.4\%$--$91.9\%$ CpG-free shares strict lower bounds.)

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
% =====================================================================

Our complete Zero-RNG audit of T2T-CHM13v2.0~\cite{Nurk2022,Rhie2023,Altemose2022} establishes four structural principles of the human chromosomal medium that could not be cleanly separated prior to gapless assembly and $V_4$-closed projection~\cite{Logvinovich2026}:

\paragraph{1. Chargaff PR2 Compliance Is Virtually Exact in Euchromatin ($99.9995\%$), While Satellite Arrays Break It Locally.}
Apparent whole-chromosome departures from Chargaff's second parity rule~\cite{Chargaff1968} in directed transition space ($\Wmm$) are overwhelmingly localized to tandem satellite blocks ($9.73\%$ of sectors). Removing these blocks via the parameter-free KI-BIC criterion~\cite{Kittler1986} suppresses chiral flow $\|\Wmm\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}}$ by $2.26\times 10^5$-fold and elevates euchromatin PR2 compliance to $99.9995\%$ ($2.08\times 10^5 : 1$ ratio of $\Wmp$ to $\Wmm$). Even the grand-pooled satellite compartment exhibits $97.85\%$ PR2 compliance despite single-chromosome drops to $56.86\%$ (\texttt{chrY}) and $57.05\%$ (\texttt{chr16}), because opposite strand orientations of HSat2/3 arrays across chromosomes partially cancel chiral $\Wmm$ under global pooling while $R$-invariant $\Wmp$ adds constructively.

\paragraph{2. Biochemical Origin and Multi-Scale Extent of the CpG-Free $\Wmp$ Arrow.}
A natural objection to any $5'\!\to 3'$ irreversibility in mammalian DNA is methylated CpG deamination ($\texttt{CG}\!\to\!\texttt{TG}/\texttt{CA}$) or nearest-neighbor dinucleotide bias. Two independent controls refute reduction to nearest-neighbor CpG mechanics: (i) $37.0\%$ of euchromatic $\|\Wmp\|^2$ (up to $57.9\%$ on \texttt{chr19}) lies strictly outside the $496$-dimensional $V_4$-closed dinucleotide screen envelope $\Pienv$, rising to $73\%$--$86\%$ at $d \ge 6\text{ bp}$; and (ii) $86.4\%$ of $\|\Wmp\|^2$ and $15/15$ top canonical orbits contain no extant \texttt{CG} dinucleotide. At the same time, two biological nuances merit explicit note: historical CpG deamination products (\texttt{TG}/\texttt{CA}) can still appear inside nominally CpG-free 6-mers (e.g., \texttt{TCC-CAG/CTG-GGA}, \texttt{TGC-AGT/ACT-GCA}), whereas pure polypurine/polypyrimidine and retrotransposon polarity motifs---specifically Alu SINE cores (\texttt{GGG-AGG/CCT-CCC}, \texttt{GCC-TCC/GGA-GGC}, \texttt{CCC-AGC/GCT-GGG}) and LINE-1/poly(A) tract boundaries (\texttt{AAA-ATT/AAT-TTT}, \texttt{ATT-TTT/AAA-AAT})---visibly drive the highest-ranked orbits and explain why Alu-rich \texttt{chr19} maximizes both $\|\Wmp\|^2$ and non-envelope share. Crucially, these motifs persist in a coherent $R$-symmetric orientation down to $171\text{ bp}$ and exhibit megabase-scale Hurst persistence ($H_{\mathrm{WLS}} \approx 0.835$) across $18/24$ chromosomes.

\paragraph{3. Resolution of Apparent Long-Range Rebounds via $\bmod\,5$ Aliasing.}
Without satellite stripping, the whole-genome multi-lag curve shows misleading rebounds at nucleosome ($147\text{ bp}$) and $\alpha$-satellite ($171\text{ bp}$) lags. Theorem~D19 (Theorem~\ref{thm:aliasing}) proves this is an arithmetic artifact of $d \bmod 5$ phase sampling on HSat2/3 $(\texttt{GGAAT})_n$, confirmed by the complete disappearance of the rebound in the $2.813\text{ Gb}$ pure-euchromatin spectrum---which instead reveals a modest $9\text{--}10\text{ bp}$ helical-turn shoulder followed by smooth decay to $171\text{ bp}$.

\paragraph{4. From Coding-Frame Syntax (Papers 1--3) to Chromosomal Physical Fields (Paper 4).}
A decisive question linking this atlas to our companion coding-sequence audits~\cite{Logvinovich2026,Logvinovich2026b} is whether whole-chromosome directional irreversibility $\Wmp$ is merely diluted spillover from protein-coding exons ($\sim 1.5\%$ of human DNA). Three independent invariants prove it is not: (i) the strict $24/24$ Hurst channel hierarchy ($H_{\mathrm{WLS}}(\Inb)=0.831$ vs.\ $H_{\mathrm{WLS}}(\Omega_{\mathrm{cond}})=0.543$, $p=1.19\times 10^{-7}$) shows that 3-bp coding frame-lock $\Omega_{\mathrm{cond}}$ behaves near white noise at megabase scales, whereas $\Wmp$ and $\Inb$ form coherent megabase domains; (ii) euchromatic $\Wmp$ retains $95.82\%$ constructive inter-chromosomal coherence across all 24 chromosomes (including gene-poor \texttt{chr4}, \texttt{chr13}, and \texttt{chrY}), with $t_{\mathrm{HAC}}=112.73$ peaking on gene-sparse \texttt{chr4}; and (iii) spaced palindromes $(u,\rho(u))$ locked by Theorem~\ref{thm:pal_lock} dominate $d \ge 6\text{ bp}$ with $1/d$ scale-free decay out to $171\text{ bp}$, revealing a duplex-wide architectural field extending far beyond reading frames.

\paragraph{5. Limitations and Scope Boundaries.}
Four boundaries govern interpretation: (a) the fixed $100\text{-kb}$ sector grid leaves $1.176\text{ Mb}$ ($1{,}175{,}501\text{ bp}$, $0.038\%$) of trailing chromosomal end-segments plus $5{,}155$ gap-masked euchromatin--satellite interface pairs outside sector-pooled partitions (totaling $1.181\text{ M}$ unpooled triplet pairs); (b) T2T-CHM13v2.0 represents a single hybrid reference ($46,\mathrm{XX}$ CHM13 + HG002 \texttt{chrY}) with consensus-modeled rDNA morphs, so population structural variation across the human pangenome remains future work; (c) whole-euchromatin FFT averaging dilutes localized nucleosome positioning, leaving only \texttt{chr16} and \texttt{chr19} below $p_{\mathrm{adj}} < 0.05$ under our strict Hann ENBW-adjusted $F$-test; and (d) the KI-BIC partition is purely sequence-intrinsic and intentionally annotation-blind, so cross-referencing with RepeatMasker, TADs, and replication-timing profiles is a natural next step.

\subsection{Falsification Criteria and Epistemic Firewall}
To maintain a strict firewall between exact mathematical invariants and mechanistic interpretation, four explicit falsification tests govern our claims: (i) any chromosome in which 1-Mb LOO Newey--West HAC fails ($t_{\mathrm{HAC}} < 3$) or $E_{\mathrm{loc}}$ ($M=1000\text{ bp}$) absorbs a major fraction of $\|\Wmp\|^2$ would refute genuine directional syntax (observed: $0/24$ failures); (ii) any euchromatin lag where satellite-stripped $\|\Wmp\|^2_{\mathrm{unb}}$ re-exhibits the $\bmod\,5$ rebound would falsify Theorem~\ref{thm:aliasing} as the sole driver of the $d=147/171\text{ bp}$ bump (observed: complete elimination across all $2.813\text{ Gb}$); (iii) a non-zero L\'evy area rate exceeding nominal $\alpha=0.05$ under circular-shift null would indicate uncontrolled autocorrelation leakage (observed: $0/24$); and (iv) no causal biological mechanism (e.g., replication vs.\ transcription vs.\ retrotransposon insertion bias) is claimed beyond the exact sequence-level decomposition reported here.
